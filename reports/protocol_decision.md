## 4. Experiment 2: solver-comparison protocol

### Representation and objective

- Use **DeltaSpline with 12 controls** on nominal CAM, via the existing `fit_parameterization`. θ is **unbounded at scale 1 mm**. This does not give the CAM anchor a continuous-collision certificate.
- This 12-control residual-on-CAM representation is specific to Experiment 2; it is not substituted for the different Experiment-1 representation below.
- `time_weight = 0.01`, `air_time_weight = 0.01`, both normalized by the immutable task's **nominal CAM cycle time**, not by an optimized or random candidate's duration.
- `reference_cycle_time_s` is derived once per task from the nominal `seed_for_task(task)` cycle timing and passed consistently to CAM and non-CAM optimizer arms. Its reference rule is `task_nominal_cam_cycle_time`.
- Legacy `w_air = 0`. Signed `loss_shift_voxels = 0.0` for the neutral objective configuration. Other objective declarations are `primary_k=40.0`, anneal start `20.0`, anneal end `120.0`, gouge/residual/holder weights `2.0 / 1.0 / 50.0`, and `holder_margin_mm = 0.0`. The holder, geometry and time/air terms retain their documented roles; negative lower objective alone cannot make an infeasible path win.

### Conditions

|---|---|---|---|
| `nominal-cam` | CAM only | 0 (one deterministic anchor) | No optimizer updates; one direct hard evaluation |

### Preserved continuation-adam defaults

Continuation-adam uses `shift="on"` and `backtracking="on"`, with the current T3.4c defaults, without introducing a second arithmetic recipe masquerading as the same experiment:

## 5. Selection, updates, and budget

For every non-nominal optimizer arm, selection retains the first hard-evaluated θ0 incumbent before any optimizer update. Strict improvement is required to displace it; a tied valid candidate does not replace θ0.

1. Start by hard-scoring the actual initialized candidate once, before updates. The ID is `<full-run-id>:init` in native accounting.
2. Count that call as one ordinary **hard-forward candidate** in total budget, not as a separate validation call.
3. Ranking is feasibility first, in declared lexicographic order: holder violation, gouge violation, residual violation, then scalar objective. Selection thresholds equal selected verdict tolerances with zero margin. Infeasible and non-rankable results are not promoted to feasible or zero-risk candidates.
4. Treat no improvement over initialization as a valid recorded outcome, not an exception to be concealed or routed through another initial path.

## 7. Experiment 1: floor-diagonal and random initialization

### Straight-line geometry

For each task:

- Compute the stock's X–Y bounding box in work coordinates. The line runs from its **minimum X–Y corner to maximum X–Y corner**, collinear with the stock-diagonal direction.
- The **minimum cutting Z** from the nominal CAM path supplies the **operation floor** for the line. This is a task-toolpath-derived minimum cutting height, not a generic unsupported home, and not arbitrary placement at the stock bottom.
- Keep a separate **safe rapid entry above the stock top**, with an actual safe rapid entry declaration for each evaluated trajectory. No direct diagonal descent from that rapid position is assumed as part of the rapid policy.
- Maintain required segment count and compatible K representation, exact-stop segment contiguity, known task work/machine frames, allowed feed limits, and pinned declared start/end points. Do not use CAM interior waypoints or return to the old `cam_start`→`cam_end` degenerate rule.

This is the D-08 addendum 4 contract, not a shortening of the CAM toolpath. Necessarily degenerate or infeasible seed construction is retained with its declaration, requested position, and failure reason, not edited into a valid substitute.

### Delta random initialization

- `parameter_space = "delta"`. `random` is per-free-vertex iid noise around **the floor-diagonal straight-line baseline**. It is not per-vertex noise around CAM and not noise in normalized CAM-fit theta coordinates.
- Distribution: independent zero-mean **Gaussian** draws per coordinate in **physical mm**, with **fraction = 0.05** of the task stock's **X–Y diagonal** as the coordinate standard deviation. This sigma is not a rejection threshold or physical clearance.
- Endpoint positions are pinned to the declared diagonal ends. Intermediate free vertices receive **independent, non-accumulating** displacements. There is no `sigma * sqrt(T)` random-walk growth.
- No automatic clipping, redrawing, resampling, seed rejection, replacement by CAM, or removal of an inconvenient draw from its declared slot. Gaussian σ magnitude alone is not physical validity evidence.
- Actual validity is determined only by machine envelope/task bounds, legal feed, safe rapid entry, contiguity, and `validate_for_task`. A valid plan is structural construction evidence, not safety, completion, manufacturing, or efficiency evidence. Invalid results retain the original seed, declaration hash, derivation, failure reason, and available deviation diagnostics.

### Seed derivation

```text
algorithm: sha256_v1
root_seed: 17
input: task_hash,declaration_hash,root_seed,seed
per_seed_rule: first_8_bytes_little_endian_uint64
```

The value **17** was supplied by the coordinator on 2026-11-10. It is not silently chosen as the common generator root from another experiment. The requested positions **0 and 1** remain the two declared random-initializer seed indices; this does not infer a further replacement draw after a failure. All requested positions/failures are retained in the ordinary `random_seed_sequence` order.

## 8. Collapse criterion

The geometry/completion clause (`geometry_or_completion_failure = true`) and hard-loss clause (`require_no_hard_loss_improvement_over_initial = true`) are both selected.

**Where the same solver's `experiment1_cam` passes the declared 0.125 mm geometry/completion verdict**, a candidate collapses when:

```text
ANY(
    geometry OR completion fails,
    residual_fraction > 2 * same-solver experiment1_cam residual_fraction + 0.01
)
AND
there is no hard improvement over that arm's own θ0
```

Rules and interpretation:

- The residual is explicitly **residual_fraction**, not a raw mm³ value, and the non-refusal expression uses `>` (gt), the multiplier **2.0**, and additive offset **0.01** in matching units.
- “No hard improvement” is exact: a strict improvement would be a lower hard loss. A tie, invalid/missing loss comparison, or no improvement does not become a claimed efficiency win. Unknown evidence must not become zero loss, zero residual, or automatic collapse freedom.
- “Experiment1_cam” means the matched plain-CAM control in the **same solver** and full-capacity representation, not a nominal anchor from a different solver/parameterization and not an exploratory CAM perturbation.
- `collapse_and_infeasibility_are_valid_recorded_outcomes = true`. Such a result is a valid negative or inefficient experimental outcome, not a tooling pass or a reason to erase the original execution.
- A recorded fine-verdict disagreement is retained. For a grouped collapse summary, mixed or unavailable D-04 verdicts are not rewritten as confident passes or confident collapse. Each case/dialect and hard initial/selected objective remain available for audit.

## 9. Comparisons and multiplicity

### Primary comparisons (Experiment 2, test split)

Each optimizer is compared against `nominal-cam`, on shared tasks, for:

1. `geometry_pass` rate, with the other D-04 pass outcomes recorded separately (`safety_pass` and `completion_pass`);
2. paired **Δcycle_time_s** on efficiency-eligible pairs;
3. paired **Δair_fraction** on efficiency-eligible pairs.

Independent units are **tasks**, not optimizer seeds. Values for `cycle_time_s` are in seconds; `air_fraction` is dimensionless. Delta values mean method minus CAM for the same task and dialect. An efficiency-eligible pair must satisfy the declared eligibility and completion rules; ineligible/missing data are not zero deltas or available successful comparisons.

The five optimizer sides for primary comparison are fixed Adam, annealed Adam, continuation-adam, hard CMA-ES, and smooth CMA-ES. The declared primary comparison multiplier is **3 × 5 = 15**.

### Secondary comparison

`continuation-adam` versus `annealed-adam` evaluates the T3.4 continuation hypothesis: whether explicit calibration/backtracking has evidence beyond the annealed baseline under the same representation and controls. It is a distinct secondary hypothesis, not a disguised extra CAM replicate or a preferred outcome.

### Uncertainty

- Confidence: **95%**. Use the existing **task-cluster bootstrap** utilities; two-sided paired **sign-flip** statistics; retain raw and Holm-adjusted p-values separately.
- Use exact paired sign-flip through 16 independent tasks, and the existing seeded Monte Carlo procedure above that, including identity-derived randomness rather than unpinned p-values.
- Current default in the audit helpers is **2000 bootstrap replicates** (an existing implementation default, not a newly measured compute budget). Do not turn it into a CI guarantee, resampling count from an experiment result, or performance awardee.
- Retain at-least-two independent-task CI semantics. A singleton task returns null CI with an explicit reason. Insufficient-pair or eligibility exclusions stay visible in the count/denominator, not in a hidden filter. Fewer than 5 tasks is descriptive, not strong generalization.
- Apply **Holm across the declared 3 × 5 primary comparisons**. Preserve the implementation's broader `all_comparisons_metrics_outcomes_and_families` scope for other declared outcome/family analyses without removing primary multiplicity. No post-hoc best seed, solver, task or dataset choice replaces this rule.
- Record failures, zero/negative differences, nulls, unsupported fidelities, and unfavourable path geometry without suppressing them.

## 12. Schema gaps (T4.2a tooling does not yet express)

**These are precisely the representational blockers identified during encoding. The coordinator chose not to extend the schema.** They are gaps in the older protocol tooling, not permission to silently weaken the coordinator's values.

1. The `$defs.condition` enum lacks **`continuation-adam`** and its typed shift/backtracking/default calibration context. Hard CMA-ES is not an alias and will not substitute for the explicitly selected continuation condition or its secondary comparison.
2. The allowed `init` values (`cam | straight_line | random`) have no distinct **`experiment1_cam`** control designation. The `init` axis is not a complete experiment registry. No `cam_perturbed` value should be inserted into that enum.
3. `execution.task_source` is fixed to **`prototype_analytic`**. The registered `suite_manifest` source in the campaign runner is not represented by that enum, and a prototype cannot stand in for this real cohort.
4. One `execution.split` and primary task cannot directly express a test-split Experiment 2 plus a separately scoped Experiment-1 dev experiment in one execution manifest. A single global `(condition, init, seed)` selector cannot silently duplicate a CAM-init arm under a distinct Exp1 representation without another role/scope distinction.
5. `dimensionality_subset` requires 1–3 explicitly bounded variants, but Experiment 1 needs 9 dev tasks, each with its own shared **`K=T`, zero-base DeltaResidual** representation. The family of full-capacity task-dependent parameter maps is not expressed by a scalar K override. An empty minimal-variant stub cannot stand in for the requested shared full-capacity construction.
6. `straight_line_init.start` and `.end` require the legacy `cam_start`/`cam_end`, while the selected floor-diagonal needs a minimum-corner → maximum-corner X–Y diagonal at nominal CAM minimum cutting Z, plus safe rapid entry. No named floor-diagonal endpoint expression exists in the legacy typed fields. `random_init` can describe delta/Gaussian behavior, but cannot reuse an old CAM-interior baseline and claim addendum 4 compliance.
7. `collapse_criterion` has multiplier/`gt`/two clause switches, but no direct typed **+0.01 additive residual offset** or same-solver CAM-pass qualifier. The exact full predicate above is normative prose. The current tool also documents that it does not parse or execute that natural-language predicate, so it is not a substitute for actual typed predicate evaluation.
8. Legacy required screen/convergence entries (`1.0 mm` and `0.25 mm`) have no `enabled=false` for the campaign-only posted policy. The frozen values instead **turn off screen/convergence** through exact executable configs. Those immutable fields cannot be silently repurposed as running phases.

The originally missing owner values `total_wall_time_s` and `random_init.seed_derivation_rule.root_seed` are now explicitly provided as **172800 s** and **17**, respectively. Those are no longer missing. They were not guessed, and no other missing timeout/calibration or topology value is being invented.

Native counts and the complete D-13 productions need their exact `run_campaign.py` configuration and Exp1 configuration. Prototype-only native validation must not be cited as evidence that those configurations can run, that all these structures are registered, or that a schema validation passed.
